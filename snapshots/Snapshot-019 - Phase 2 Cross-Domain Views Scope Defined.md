## Decisions

### DEC-022
Cross-Domain Views scope for Phase 2 is defined and human-confirmed.
(Recorded in full in decisions.md.) Discovery = persona-anchored,
domain-keyed. A Cross-Domain View is a named multi-domain composition
rendered on an Operating Surface; a surface may host more than one view;
views are not portal entries. Granularity = one view per persona
decision-context (canon-worthy). Naming = decision/subject + "View"
(canon-worthy). Inventory = 7 firm views (Client Brain View, Lead Context
View, Performance & Analytics View, Team Oversight View, Engagement Health
View [host deferred], Client Engagement Summary View [host deferred],
Contractor Assignment-in-Context View); System Administrator none. Flag
resolutions: Reports & Analytics Surface → both (hosts the Performance &
Analytics View and the Team Oversight View, separately named); Team
Performance / Oversight Surface → pure Cross-Domain View. Entitlement
deferred to the Permission Matrix; views consume only (no own/compute).
Records scope only; does not write canon, does not finalize deferred hosts,
does not populate the Permission Matrix.
